# Product marketing context (pointer)

Some third-party skills in `.claude/skills/ext-*` look for this file. In this repo the source of truth is `knowledge/`.
Do not copy facts here; read these files instead, in this order:

1. `knowledge/INDEX.md`: map of everything the team knows.
2. `knowledge/products/vutty.md` and `knowledge/products/vutidy.md`: what each app does, price, signing status, release state.
3. `knowledge/brand/brand-book.md`: positioning, one-line description, voice, words to use and avoid.
4. `knowledge/market/competitors.md`: competitors with sourced prices and dates.
5. `knowledge/rules/compliance.md`: claims we must not make.
6. `knowledge/learnings/LEARNINGS.md`: what we already learned (newest first).
